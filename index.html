✦ OUR PHILOSOPHY

Good coffee.
Nothing complicated.

We believe coffee doesn't need to be complicated. Just good beans, careful roasting and a little time. We source directly from sustainable shade-grown micro-farms, roast each lot to unlock its native terroir, and serve every cup with unhurried warmth.

Small-Batch Roasting

5kg cast-iron drum roaster allows pinpoint thermal control for each batch.

Direct Fair Trade

Paying 40% above fair-trade floor directly to multigenerational farm families.

Pure Mountain Water

Filtered through 5-stage reverse osmosis and re-mineralized for optimal extraction.

Zero Single-Use Waste

100% plant-derived compostable cups or bring your ceramic cup for 10% off.
